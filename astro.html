Computational Astrophysics and Meteorology

In my astrophysics and meteorology projects, I combine numerical PDE-based simulations with modern AI/ML approaches to explore both cosmic and climate systems. On one side, I’ve built Fortran solvers for 2D convection, barotropic vorticity, and advection–diffusion, alongside Python pipelines for visualization and data analysis. On the other, I’ve analyzed ERA5/ERA5-Land reanalysis datasets to study long-term potential vorticity and solar PV potential changes across Europe (1950–2010), producing climatology maps and animated comparisons. Complementing these physics-based methods, I also experiment with machine learning models such as CNNs, GNNs, and SVMs to classify galaxies, interpret astronomical datasets, and extract patterns from large-scale simulations. Together, these projects reflect my goal of uniting physics-grounded models with AI-driven analytics to advance our understanding of both celestial phenomena and climate variability.

2D PDE Models

Developed modular Fortran solvers for simulating classical 2D PDE-based fluid dynamics problems, supported by Python utilities for NetCDF I/O and visualization. Models include moist convection, barotropic vorticity, and scalar advection–diffusion.

PV Potential and Climatology Changes Across Europe (1950–2010)

Analysis and visualization of potential vorticity (PV) and solar photovoltaic (PV) potential changes across Europe, comparing the decades 1950–1960 and 2000–2010. Includes estimates of the land area required to meet Europe’s energy demand using PV panels.

Galaxy Image Reconstruction with U-Net

Applying a U-Net deep learning model to reconstruct detailed galaxy images from DES/DECaLS datasets degraded by synthetic Gaussian noise. Benchmarks include classical DCT/DWT pipelines enhanced with iterative shrinkage–thresholding (ISTA).

Low-Rank Galaxy Images

Dimensionality reduction of spiral galaxy images from DESI Legacy Surveys using low-rank approximations for compression and fast feature extraction across g, r, z bands.

05

Spatial Distribution Analysis of Galaxies (SDSS)

Data-driven notebooks analyzing ~320k SDSS galaxies: redshift distributions, color–magnitude relations, luminosity statistics, and angular two-point correlation functions using Landy–Szalay estimators.

06

Cosmology-Supernova-Analysis: Estimating the Universe’s Age and the Hubble Constant

This project centers on analyzing Type Ia supernovae from the Supernova Cosmology Project’s “Union2.1” dataset to estimate the Hubble constant and the age of the Universe. Type Ia supernovae, known as standard candles in cosmology, allow for precise distance measurements to their host galaxies due to their nearly constant peak luminosity. The analysis employs linear regression techniques to explore the dataset and derive significant cosmological parameters.

Galaxy Reconstruction Using ISTA

Applied the Iterative Soft Thresholding Algorithm (ISTA) with compressed sensing to reconstruct spiral galaxy images from the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys (DECaLS), degraded with Gaussian noise and subsampling. Benchmarked against DCT and DWT transforms for recovery quality.

GAN Galaxy Images

This project harnesses the power of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to generate synthetic images of galaxies based on the Galaxy10 DECals dataset. By training a GAN model, the project aims to produce new, realistic images of galaxies across various morphological classes, enhancing our understanding of galaxy features and distributions.

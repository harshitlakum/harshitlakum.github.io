01

Field-Controlled Assembly and Structural Transitions in Shifted-Dipole Trimer Clusters

Brownian-dynamics simulations in ESPResSo examine how a dimensionless dipole shift drives ring→chain transitions in Janus trimers. Using an orientational order parameter S, results show thermal fluctuations shift/expand transition windows relative to athermal ground-state predictions, highlighting finite-temperature effects in real systems.

  • Affiliation: Department of Physics, Universität Wien
  • Date: July 6, 2025
  • Includes: Abstract, Model, Methods, Results, Conclusions, Bibliography, ESPResSo code snippet

02

U-Net for Image Reconstruction (Denoising)

Evaluates U-Net for denoising galaxy images from DECaLS corrupted with Gaussian noise and compares against DCT/DWT baselines enhanced by ISTA. Initial experiments suggest U-Net outperforms classical transforms on this dataset, with performance sensitivity to data characteristics. Includes a concise mathematical overview of the encoder–decoder.

Lung Nodule Classification, Detection, and Segmentation (Minor Project Report)

A medical imaging workflow using preprocessing (CLAHE), random-walk segmentation, and classification baselines, with a forward-looking note on improving accuracy via gradient-boosting (XGBoost). Includes department certificates, acknowledgements, and an abstract.

Counting Objects from Live Video Stream (Major Project Report)

Applied computer vision pipeline with preprocessing, detection, and segmentation to count vehicles from live video. Discusses method choices, limitations, and outlines a future direction using modern learning-based detectors and boosted models.
